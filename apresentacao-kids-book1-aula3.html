👂 Watch Out!

Some nationalities are similar in English and Portuguese, but others change a lot!

✅ Similar
🇧🇷 Brazilian → Brasileiro
🇦🇷 Argentinian → Argentino
🇮🇹 Italian → Italiano
🇨🇦 Canadian → Canadense
⚠️ Very different
🇬🇧 British → Britânico
🇫🇷 French → Francês
🇯🇵 Japanese → Japonês
🇺🇸 American → Americano
