05 Reading

Mia's routine

Meet Mia. She lives in a small village in England. Mia loves to eat apples every day. She also likes to drink fresh water.

In the morning, Mia goes to school. She studies and listens to her teacher. After school, she runs to the park. She loves to run and play with her friends.

In the evening, Mia goes back home. She helps her mom cook dinner. They make yummy pasta together. Then, Mia eats her favourite meal.

Before bedtime, Mia reads a storybook. She loves to write and read stories. Then, she goes to her cozy bed and sleeps peacefully.

On weekends, Mia and her friends go to the park. They sing and dance together. They also watch the birds in the trees.

Mia enjoys her simple life in the village. She talks and waits for her friends to come over. She is happy every day.
