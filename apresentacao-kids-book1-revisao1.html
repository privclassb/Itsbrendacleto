Change into Negatives 🔄

Say each sentence in the negative!

1 – She likes Art.
She doesn't like Art.

2 – He plays Soccer.
He doesn't play Soccer.

3 – They study English.
They don't study English.

4 – She has a dog.
She doesn't have a dog.

5 – He eats Pizza.
He doesn't eat Pizza.

6 – They play Basketball.
They don't play Basketball.

7 – She drinks Juice.
She doesn't drink Juice.

8 – He studies Math.
He doesn't study Math.

Change into Interrogatives ❓

Say each sentence as a question!

1 – She likes Music.
Does she like Music?

2 – He plays Tennis.
Does he play Tennis?

3 – They have a cat.
Do they have a cat?

4 – She studies Science.
Does she study Science?

5 – He eats Bread.
Does he eat Bread?

6 – They drink Soda.
Do they drink Soda?

7 – She plays Volleyball.
Does she play Volleyball?

8 – He has a sister.
Does he have a sister?
