🧩 English is like a puzzle!

Every part of the sentence is a piece. Together they make the message!

TO EAT = comer
I eat pizza 🍕
You eat bread 🍞
TO DRINK = beber
I drink juice 🧃
You drink milk 🥛
Subject
I / You
Verb
eat / drink
Food / Drink
🍕 pizza / 🧃 juice
