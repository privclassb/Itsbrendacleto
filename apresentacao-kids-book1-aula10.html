Fill in the Gaps

Complete the sentences. Say your answers out loud!

Ex.: She has (have) breakfast at 7

1 – He has (have) lunch at school

2 – She doesn't have (not have) dinner at home

3 – Do you have breakfast at home?

4 – Does he have dinner with his family?

5 – They have (have) breakfast at 8
