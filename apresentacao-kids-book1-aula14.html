📍 Where does it go?

Frequency words and weekly phrases have different places!

Frequency word → BEFORE the verb
She often walks the dog.
Weekly phrase → at the END
She walks the dog twice a week.
Question
How often does he read a book?
Answer
He reads a book three times a week.
💡 always = every day
