🔍 Get ready: Mystery Tiles!

How to play

1
A picture is hiding behind 9 tiles.
2
Say a number to open a tile: "Number five, please!"
3
Guess what it is. Say it in English!
4
Fewer tiles open = more points: 1-3 tiles = 3 ⭐ · 4-6 = 2 ⭐ · 7-9 = 1 ⭐
🔍 Game 1: Mystery TilesSay a number to open a tile. Fewer tiles = more points!

💣 Get ready: Category Blitz!

How to play

1
You see a category (animals, foods, sports...).
2
You have 30 seconds!
3
Say as many English words as you can. Example: Animals → dog, cat, lion!
4
Each word = 1 point. No repeated words. No Portuguese! 🙅
💣 Game 2: Category Blitz30 seconds. Say as many words as you can!

🧠 Get ready: Memory Match!

How to play

1
First, look at all the cards for 5 seconds. Remember them! 👀
2
Then the cards turn over. Say two numbers: "Number 3 and number 9!"
3
Find a pair: picture + word (🍎 = apple).
4
Pair = 1 point. Say the word and a sentence: "I like pizza!"
🧠 Game 4: Memory MatchFind the pairs: picture + word!

🪅 Get ready: Save the Piñata!

How to play

1
You see a hint and empty spaces: _ _ _ _ _
2
Say a letter in English: A = "ay", B = "bee", C = "see"...
3
Right letter: it appears! ✅ Wrong letter: the piñata gets hit! 💥
4
6 hits = the piñata breaks. Complete the word = 1 point 🍬
🪅 Game 5: Save the PiñataGuess the letters. 6 mistakes and the piñata breaks!
