💛 Want and 💙 Need

I want = eu quero · I need = eu preciso

want = quero
I want a new tablet.
need = preciso
I need my towel for swimming class.
want + to + verb
I want to play basketball.
need + to + verb
She needs to clean her room.
💡 he / she → wants · needs  |  ❌ I don't want · She doesn't need
