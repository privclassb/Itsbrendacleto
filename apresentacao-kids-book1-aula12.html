📊 Adverbs of Frequency

They go BEFORE the main verb!

100%
Always
I always brush my teeth before bed.
~75%
Usually
She usually walks the dog after school.
~25%
Sometimes
We sometimes watch TV at night.
0%
Never
He never cleans his room on Sundays.
