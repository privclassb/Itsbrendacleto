Part 1: The big trip

🏴
England
Hard life at home
The Pilgrims want a new life.
🚢
The Mayflower
A big ship
About 100 people sail across the ocean.
⏳
66 days
A long, hard trip
There are big waves and bad weather.
🌎
America
A new land
They arrive in November and call it Plymouth.

❄️ The First Winter · 1620

Part 2: A hard time

🥶
Cold
A very cold winter
The houses are not ready.
🍞
No food
Not enough food
Many Pilgrims are sick.
🤝
New friends
The Wampanoag people
They live in this land. They help the Pilgrims!
🧑‍🌾
Squanto
A kind teacher

🍂 The First Feast · 1621

Part 3: Friends eat together

🌽
Autumn 1621
A good harvest
Now there is food for everybody!
👥
Together
Pilgrims and Wampanoag
About 50 Pilgrims and 90 Wampanoag people.
🎉
Three days
A big celebration
They eat, play games and say thank you.
🦌
The food
Corn, fish, birds, deer
Maybe turkey too!

🧡 I'm Thankful For...

thankful = grato · I'm = I am

I'm = I am
I'm thankful for my family.
He's / She's
She's thankful for her dog.
We're / They're
We're thankful for our friends.
❌ not
I'm not thankful for green beans. 😄
He isn't thankful for homework.
❓ What are you thankful for?  |  Are you thankful for your pet? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
