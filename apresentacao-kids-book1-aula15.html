📍 How do we say it?

Classes with have / has. Objects with take + my, his, her, our!

I / you / we / they → have
I have ballet class twice a week.
he / she → has
She has ballet class twice a week.
I → my
I take my notebook to English class.
she → her
She takes her ballet shoes to ballet class.
💡 I → my · he → his · she → her · we → our
